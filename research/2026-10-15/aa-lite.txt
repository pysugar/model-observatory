Comparison Summary
Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably fast and fairly concise. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.
Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite scores 22 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 13). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 59M tokens, which is fairly concise in comparison to the median of 94M.
Pricing for Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is $0.30 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $0.25) and $2.50 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $0.87). On average, it costs $0.19 per task to evaluate Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite on the Intelligence Index.
At 438 tokens per second, Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite is notably fast (108).

Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $0.25) and $2.50 per 1M output tokens (at the higher end, median: $0.87), based on Google's API.
What is Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite API pricing?
Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens and $2.50 per 1M output tokens (based on Google's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $0.33 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider.
